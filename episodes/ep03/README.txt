EBO TEACHES MONEY — Ep 03: How to Stay Poor (Adobe Animate file)

Stage: 1920 x 1080 (16:9 long-form), 30 fps, 5058 frames (2 min 48.6 s)

Ebo sarcastically "teaches" six habits that keep you broke: watch TV all day, no hobbies,
no passion, stay in your comfort zone, wait for magic, blame everyone else. The cast act
each one out, then Ebo flips it into what to do instead. Each habit opens on a chapter card.
On-screen text is kept to a minimum.

LAYERS (top to bottom)
  Captions    - editable text in caption pills at the bottom of the frame (Library > Captions)
  Graphics    - kept light: the title, chapter cards, the FLIP IT marker, one speech
                bubble and the end card, as editable text + vector shapes (Library > Graphics).
                The points themselves are acted out by the cast, not shown as infographics.
  Props front - props the characters hold: popcorn, remote, phone, brush, notebook,
                lottery ticket, cash, sneakers
  Ebo, Christy, Mica, Ama, Obuor
              - each plays its animated rig symbol, e.g. Library > Ebo > "Ebo – Ep03".
                Walking and running use your own cycles (cast/cycles).
  Props       - easel and portrait, laptop, hamster wheel, crystal ball, candles, calendar:
                editable vector symbols in the hand-drawn style (Library > Props)
  Background  - the sets only (studio, living room, bedroom, office, sneaker room, the
                dark chapter-card room) as editable vector symbols, one per set and camera
                framing (Library > Background). Each one is also in backgrounds/ as an
                .svg file you can open in Illustrator or import.

ADD THE VOICE (1 minute)
  1. File > Import > Import to Library... > choose Ebo_Ep03_voice.mp3
  2. Insert a new layer named "Voice", select frame 1
  3. In Properties > Sound, pick Ebo_Ep03_voice.mp3 and set Sync to "Stream"
  The animation is already timed to this file from frame 1.

NOTES
  - Text uses Arial Black / Arial Bold; outlines are Glow filters on the text.
  - In the video the sets are slightly blurred for depth; in Animate they are sharp.
  - Props in the video have a light crayon grain; in Animate they keep the wavy hand-drawn
    outlines but use flat fills.
  - Built by script and checked by re-rendering it outside Animate; please open it
    in Animate and report anything that looks off.

FACTS USED
  - US adults watch about 2.8 hours of TV a day (Bureau of Labor Statistics, American Time
    Use Survey): "almost three hours", "over a thousand hours a year".
  - A full-time year is about 2,000 working hours.
  - Odds of a big US jackpot are about 1 in 292 million (Powerball): "about one in three
    hundred million".
