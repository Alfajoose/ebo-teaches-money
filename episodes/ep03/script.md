# Ebo Teaches Money — Ep 03: How to Stay Poor (draft)

16:9, about 3½ minutes. Ebo is sarcastic: he "teaches" six habits that keep you broke, the cast acts each one out, then he flips it into what to do instead. No lesson numbers: each habit gets a title card.

---

**Hook**
Want to stay poor forever? Good news. It's easy. Just follow these six habits. And if you'd rather get rich... do the exact opposite.

**WATCH TV ALL DAY**
Watch TV all day. The average person watches almost three hours a day. That's over a thousand hours a year. A full-time job is about two thousand. So you're spending half a work year... on the couch.
Ama has seen every episode of everything. Her bank account has seen nothing.
*Flip it:* take one hour back a day. That's three hundred and sixty-five hours a year to learn a skill, start a side hustle, or build something people pay for.

**NO HOBBIES**
Have no hobbies. Hobbies are dangerous. They make you good at things.
Christy draws every evening. Two years later, people pay her fifty dollars a portrait. Mica, meanwhile, scrolls. Same phone. Different result.
*Flip it:* pick one thing you enjoy making, fixing or doing. Do it every week. Skills grow quietly... then they start paying.

**NO PASSION**
Have no passion. Wake up, do the minimum, go back to sleep. Never ask what you care about, because passion makes you work harder, notice problems, and keep going when it's boring.
Obuor loves sneakers. He knows every release date. That's not wasted time... that's market research.
*Flip it:* write down three things you could talk about for an hour. Then ask: who would pay for help with this?

**STAY IN YOUR COMFORT ZONE**
Stay in your comfort zone. Same job. Same routine. Same excuses. Never ask for a raise, never apply anywhere new, never try selling anything.
Comfortable feels safe. But a hamster on a wheel is comfortable too. It runs all day... and goes nowhere.
*Flip it:* do one uncomfortable thing a week. Send the email. Ask for the raise. Post the first video.

**WAIT FOR MAGIC**
Wait for magic. The lottery. A rich uncle. A video that just goes viral by itself.
The odds of winning a big lottery jackpot are about one in three hundred million. You are more likely to get struck by lightning.
*Flip it:* make your own luck. Small bets, done often. Every try is another ticket, and these tickets actually win.

**BLAME EVERYONE ELSE**
Blame everyone else. The economy. Your boss. Your parents. The weather. Blaming feels great, because it means nothing is your job. It also means nothing changes.
*Flip it:* ask one question. What part of this can I control? Start there.

**Wrap-up**
So here's the recipe to stay poor: TV all day. No hobbies. No passion. Stay comfortable. Wait for magic. Blame everyone.
And here's the recipe to get rich: do the opposite, one small habit at a time. Start today.
