# Ebo Teaches Money, Ep 07: Wants vs. Needs

Status: draft v4 for approval. Vertical YouTube Short, about 45 to 50 seconds.
Back to Ebo teaching: he's alone and talks to us the whole way (no conversation). He's also the example, and the joke is on him (self-roast, like Ep4).
House rules: no lesson numbers (unnumbered handwritten cards that stack up), the acting carries it, still camera with hard cuts, Ebo faces us and stands, hands gesture on the beat.
Props he handles: his phone (bank app), his budget list (paper), three gold chains he wears from the first frame.

## Cold open
(Ebo, three gold chains on, staring at his bank app)
EBO: I can't make rent this month. I don't get it.
(looks down at the chains. Back up at us. Long beat.)
EBO: …Okay. I get it.

## Hook
EBO (to camera): A lot of us aren't broke because we earn too little. We're broke because our wants are dressed up as needs.

## The vanish test
(card: Would you HAVE to replace it?)
EBO: Here's the test. If it vanished tomorrow, would you have to replace it?
EBO (holds up his list, ticking): Rent? Yes. Food? Yes. Getting to work? Yes.
EBO (lifts the chains, long beat): …These? No.

## Watch the sneaky ones
(card: Watch the sneaky ones)
EBO: And watch the sneaky ones. A need with a want hiding inside.
EBO: You need food. You want delivery.
EBO (holds up his phone): You need a phone. You want the new one. Every year.
EBO: You need clothes. You want the brand.

## The fix
(card: Needs first. Wants on purpose.)
EBO: So pay your needs first. Then whatever's left, pick your wants on purpose. And enjoy them. No guilt.
EBO (takes two chains off, keeps one, slow grin): This one? Paid for. With want money.
(hard cut back to the cold open: three chains, "I can't make rent")
