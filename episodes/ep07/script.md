# Ebo Teaches Money, Ep 07: Wants vs. Needs

Status: draft v3 for approval (new cold open: broke in gold chains). Vertical YouTube Short, about 50 seconds.
Ebo is the one who gets caught (self-roast, like Ep4): his "needs" list is mostly wants. Ama reads it out.
v2: the two definition cards are replaced by one test he acts out with the props; the fix is one card; it ends on the punchline and loops.
House rules: no lesson numbers (unnumbered handwritten cards that stack up), the acting carries it, still camera with hard cuts, Ebo faces us and stands.
Props he handles: his phone (bank app), his budget list (paper), three gold chains he wears from the first frame.

## Cold open
(Ebo, three gold chains on, staring at his bank app)
EBO: I can't make rent this month. I don't get it.
(Ama looks at the chains. Looks at him.)
AMA: …You don't get it?
EBO (to camera): Okay. We need to talk about wants versus needs.

## The vanish test
(card: Would you HAVE to replace it?)
EBO: Here's the test. If it vanished tomorrow, would you have to replace it?
EBO (holds up the list): Rent? Yes. Or I'm living in my car.
EBO (lifts the chains, long beat): …The chains? No.

## Watch the sneaky ones
(card: Watch the sneaky ones)
EBO: And watch the sneaky ones. You need food. You want delivery.
EBO (taps his phone): You need a phone. You want the new one. Every year.
AMA (running a finger down his list): Delivery. New phone. Gold chains.
EBO: …Wants.
AMA: All of them?
EBO (deflates): All of them.

## The fix
(card: Needs first. Wants on purpose.)
EBO (to camera): So: needs first. Then pick your wants on purpose. No guilt.
AMA: And the chains?
EBO (takes two off, keeps one, slow grin): This one? Paid for. With want money.
(hard cut back to the cold open: three chains, "I can't make rent")
