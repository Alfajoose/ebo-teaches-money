# Ebo Teaches Money, Ep 07: Wants vs. Needs

Status: draft v5 for approval. Vertical, about 1:15 to 1:25 (no longer squeezed to 45s).
Ebo teaching: he's alone and talks to us the whole way (no conversation). He's also the example, and the joke is on him (self-roast, like Ep4).
House rules: no lesson numbers (unnumbered handwritten cards that stack up), the acting carries it, still camera with hard cuts, Ebo faces us and stands, hands gesture on the beat.
Props he handles: his phone (bank app, streaming apps), his budget list (paper), three gold chains he wears from the first frame.

## Cold open
(Ebo, three gold chains on, staring at his bank app)
EBO: I can't make rent this month. I don't get it.
(looks down at the chains. Back up at us. Long beat.)
EBO: …Okay. I get it.

## Hook
EBO (to camera): A lot of us aren't broke because we earn too little. We're broke because our wants are dressed up as needs.
EBO: So let's learn to tell them apart. Without giving up everything fun.

## The vanish test
(card: Would you HAVE to replace it?)
EBO: Here's the test. If it vanished tomorrow, would you have to replace it?
EBO (holds up his list, ticking): Rent? Yes. Food? Yes. Getting to work? Yes.
EBO (lifts the chains, long beat): …These? No.

## Watch the sneaky ones
(card: Watch the sneaky ones)
EBO: Now watch the sneaky ones. A need with a want hiding inside.
EBO: You need food. You want delivery.
EBO (holds up his phone): You need a phone. You want the new one. Every year.
EBO: You need clothes. You want the brand.
EBO (scrolling, slowing down): And the sneakiest of all: subscriptions. Four streaming apps. …I watch one.

## Wants can wait
(card: Wants can wait)
EBO: Here's the thing. Needs are urgent. Wants can wait.
EBO: So give every want a one-day pause. Still want it tomorrow? Fine, think about it.
EBO (touches the chains): Forgot about it by tomorrow? It was never that important.

## Needs first. Wants on purpose.
(card: Needs first. Wants on purpose.)
EBO: So when the money lands, pay your needs first. Rent, food, transport. Done.
EBO: Then give your wants a fixed amount. Your fun money. Spend it however you like, no guilt.
EBO: When it's gone, it's gone. Until next month.

## The close
EBO (to camera): Wants aren't the enemy. Wants with no limit are.
EBO (takes two chains off, keeps one, slow grin): This one? Paid for. With want money.
(hard cut back to the cold open: three chains, "I can't make rent")
