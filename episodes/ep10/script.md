# Ep 10 — Marry a Woman Who…

Draft 2, 2026-11-10. Topic from the user (an image post by @tatendanhema — credit it in the caption).
Faith side left out; addressed to men. Draft 1 made money the thread; the user didn't like money advice
forced in and made specific, so this is a pure life lesson (draft 1 is in git). Voice: Ebo (Dan), confident throughout.

**The question:** why does who you marry decide the life you build?
**The answer (one picture):** marriage is one house, two builders. Everything each of you does is a brick or a crack.
Each quality is explained by what it does to that house.

| # | Ebo says | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who you marry decides what kind of life you build. | Ebo, front on, holding a single brick. |
| 2 | Because marriage is one house, and two people building it. | An empty plot. Ebo and his wife walk in from opposite sides and meet in the middle. |
| 3 | Everything you each do is either a brick, or a crack. | A kind word becomes a brick and lands in the wall. An eye-roll becomes a crack that zigzags across it. |
| 4 | So marry a woman who works with willing hands. When you both build, the walls go up fast. When one builds and one watches, the builder gets tired. | Both lay bricks side by side, the wall shoots up. Then: Ebo alone, sweating, while the wall barely grows. |
| 5 | Marry a woman who builds a home, not just decorates a house. A house can look perfect outside and feel empty inside. | A shiny house front, like a film set… it tips over: nothing behind it. |
| 6 | Marry a woman who handles conflict with grace. Every couple argues. The question is: does the argument fix the leak, or knock down a wall? | Rain, a leak. Version A: they patch it together. Version B: they shout and a wall falls over. |
| 7 | Marry a woman who respects you, in person and online. Problems you solve at home stay small. Problems you post online get bigger. | A small crack inside the house… then a phone posting it, and the crack grows across the whole wall. |
| 8 | And marry a woman who takes care of herself, inside and out. You can't build a strong house with a worn-out builder. | She waters a plant at the window, rests, laughs; the house glows warmer. |
| 9 | Get this right, and you build a home that stands for life. Get it wrong, and no amount of decorating holds it up. | The finished house, lights on… vs a cracked one with curtains flapping. |
| 10 | And remember: she's choosing a builder too. Be the man worth building with. | Ebo picks up a brick, wipes his hands, smiles at camera. |
