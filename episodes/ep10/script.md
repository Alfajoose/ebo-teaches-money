# Ep 10 — Marry a Woman Who… (why who you marry matters more than what you earn)

Draft 1, 2026-11-10. Topic from the user (an image post by @tatendanhema — credit it in the caption).
Faith side left out; addressed to men; money is the thread. Voice: Ebo (Dan), confident throughout.

**The question:** why does who you marry matter more than what you earn?
**The answer (one picture):** marriage is one house, two builders. Every paycheck is a brick; every bad habit is a crack.
Each quality is explained by what it does to that house.

| # | Ebo says | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Here's something nobody tells you: who you marry matters more than what you earn. | Ebo, front on. A wedding ring drops onto a stack of cash and outweighs it, like a scale tipping. |
| 2 | Because once you're married, there's one house, and two people building it. | An empty plot. Ebo and his wife walk in from opposite sides and meet in the middle. |
| 3 | Every paycheck is a brick. Every bad habit is a crack. | A paycheck turns into a brick and lands in the wall. Then a crack zigzags across it. |
| 4 | So marry a woman who works with willing hands. Two people building, and the walls go up twice as fast. | Both lay bricks side by side; the wall shoots up. |
| 5 | Marry a woman who builds a home, not just decorates a house. If every paycheck goes on curtains, the walls never get finished. | Same house, other ending: no walls, just fancy curtains hanging from nothing, swaying in the wind. |
| 6 | Marry a woman who handles conflict with grace. Every couple fights about money. The question is: does the fight fix the leak, or knock down a wall? | Rain, a leak in the roof. Version A: they patch it together. Version B: they argue and a wall falls over. |
| 7 | Marry a woman who respects you, in person and online. Problems you solve at home stay small. Problems you post online get bigger. | A small crack inside the house… then a phone with the crack posted on it, and the crack grows across the whole wall. |
| 8 | And marry a woman who takes care of herself. One hospital stay can cost more than a year of savings. | A hospital bill blows in and knocks bricks out of the savings pile. |
| 9 | Get this right, and two average incomes build a strong home. | The finished house: solid, lights on. |
| 10 | Get it wrong, and even a big salary can't hold the walls up. | A big paycheck brick on a cracked wall; the wall crumbles anyway. |
| 11 | And remember: she's choosing a builder too. Be the man worth building with. | Ebo picks up a brick, wipes his hands, smiles at camera. |
