Shot sizes: **EWS** extreme wide, **WS** wide, **MS** medium (waist-up), **CU** close-up (head and shoulders), **ECU** extreme close-up (face or prop detail), **2S** two-shot, **OTS** over-the-shoulder.
Camera: **static**, **push-in**, **pull-out**, **pan/track** (direction), **whip-pan**, **snap-zoom**, **follow**. The camera may zoom; characters and props never scale.
Transitions: **cut** (default), **match cut**, **whip** (the motion carries into the next shot).

## Checks before animating
- Each scene has an establishing shot, then closer coverage, with reaction shots where a joke lands.
- No two adjacent shots share the same size and angle, unless it's a deliberate jump cut.
- Each camera move has a reason: reveal, emphasis, follow or comedy.
- Points are acted out by the cast. Text on screen is limited to titles, chapter cards and the odd speech bubble.
- Every prop and set needed is listed, so props and backgrounds can be built separately.
