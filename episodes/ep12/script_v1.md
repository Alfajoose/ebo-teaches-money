# Ep 12 — Why Saying Sorry Too Much Makes People Respect You Less

Draft 1, 2026-11-19. The 5-beat format (proposed 2026-11-11): the mistake (skit) → "Don't be that guy." →
"Here's the fix." → the replay, done right → the close. ~35 s, vertical. Cast: Ebo and Mica. (No Ghanaian references.)

**The one idea:** say sorry for everything and people start seeing you as someone who's always messing up.
**The fix:** swap "sorry" for "thank you".

| # | Beat | Who | Line | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mistake | EBO | Sorry! Sorry I'm late, sorry, the traffic was— sorry! | Ebo bursts into a café, late. Mica at a table, arms crossed. Ebo bumps a chair on the last "sorry". |
| 2 | Mistake | EBO | *(to the chair)* …Sorry. | He apologises to the chair. Mica checks his watch. A cricket chirps. |
| 3 | Don't be that guy | EBO | Don't be that guy. Say sorry for everything, and people start seeing you as someone who's always messing up. | Ebo to camera. |
| 4 | Here's the fix | EBO | Here's the fix. Swap "sorry"… for "thank you". | Pop-ups: **sorry** (crossed out) → **thank you** |
| 5 | Replay | EBO | Thanks for waiting, man! | Same café, same late entrance. Ebo smiling, relaxed. |
| 6 | Replay | MICA | No worries! I got you a drink. | Mica smiles and slides a cup across. |
| 7 | Replay | EBO | *(bumps the chair; beat)* …Thanks, chair. | The same chair. Mica laughs. |
| 8 | Close | EBO | "Sorry" makes it about your mistake. "Thank you" makes it about them. | Ebo to camera, close. |
