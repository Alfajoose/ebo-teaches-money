# Ebo Teaches Money — Ep 04: The Ultimate Guide to Staying Broke

16:9 long-form, about 4:20. Narrator: Ebo (alone on screen, playing both the host and the guy doing every habit). Written by the channel owner; edits agreed in chat: no spoken rule numbers (chapter cards carry the structure), one real number in scenes 2 and 6, "I certainly don't." set-up, twelve hours in scene 3, a sincere recap and "Subscribe for more." at the end.

---

**SCENE 1 — INTRODUCTION**
Congratulations! You've decided you want to stay poor. And lucky for you, it's actually pretty easy.
You don't need a degree. You don't need experience. You don't even need to leave your house.
All you need is a comfortable couch, a smartphone, and an extraordinary ability to make terrible decisions.
Now, obviously, life isn't fair. Some people start with way more opportunities than others.
But if your goal is to make your situation even worse… Oh boy. Have I got some tips for you.
Welcome to the ultimate guide to staying broke.

**SCENE 2 — WATCH TV ALL DAY**
Watch television. Lots of television. And when I say lots, I mean LOTS.
The average person already watches almost three hours a day. Rookie numbers.
Wake up in the morning? Television. Eating breakfast? Television. Supposed to be working on something important? You guessed it. Television!
And if you're feeling particularly ambitious, switch to your phone. Because why waste eight hours watching television when you can waste twelve hours scrolling through videos of strangers doing absolutely nothing?
Remember, the goal isn't to relax. Relaxing is perfectly normal. The goal is to make sure entertainment becomes your entire personality.
And whatever you do… never ask yourself where all that time went. I certainly don't.

**SCENE 3 — HAVE NO HOBBIES**
Avoid hobbies. Especially hobbies that teach you something.
Drawing? Too much effort. Writing? Sounds exhausting. Learning to code? Absolutely disgusting.
Why would you spend your free time developing a skill when you could spend twelve hours watching someone else develop theirs?
And please, don't even think about trying something new. You might discover you're actually good at it. And then what? You might improve. You might meet interesting people. You might even find an opportunity. Terrifying.
Better to stay exactly where you are.

**SCENE 4 — HAVE NO PASSION**
Never pursue anything you care about.
Have a dream? Ignore it. Got an idea? Forget it. Want to start a project? Tell yourself you'll do it next Monday.
And when Monday comes… well, obviously, you meant next Monday.
This is called strategic procrastination. Very advanced stuff.
Eventually, you'll become so good at planning things that you'll never actually have to do anything.
You'll have seventeen unfinished ideas, forty motivational screenshots, and absolutely nothing to show for them.
But hey… at least your phone gallery looks inspirational.

**SCENE 5 — STAY IN YOUR COMFORT ZONE**
Stay comfortable. At all costs.
Never apply for a better opportunity. What if they reject you? Never try learning something difficult. What if you fail? And definitely never ask questions. What if somebody thinks you're stupid?
See, the trick is to avoid every situation where you might feel uncomfortable. That way, you'll never experience embarrassment. Or rejection. Or failure.
Of course, you might also miss opportunities to grow. But that's a small price to pay for never having to try.
Besides… your comfort zone has snacks.

**SCENE 6 — WAIT FOR MAGIC**
Wait for a miracle. Don't make a plan. Don't build useful skills. Don't take action.
Just sit there and wait for the universe to recognize your incredible potential.
Maybe a millionaire will randomly knock on your door. Maybe someone will offer you your dream job while you're sleeping. Maybe a suitcase full of money will fall from the sky.
The odds of winning a big lottery jackpot are about one in three hundred million. Practically guaranteed.
And if none of those things happen? Don't worry. Just keep waiting.
After all, nothing says financial strategy quite like staring at the ceiling and hoping for the best.

**SCENE 7 — BLAME EVERYONE ELSE**
And finally… blame everybody.
Your boss. Your parents. Your friends. The government. The weather. That guy who looked at you funny in 2017. Everybody!
Now, some problems really are outside your control. But that's no reason to examine the things you actually can change.
Because the moment you take responsibility for one small decision… you might have to do something about it. And we certainly can't have that.

**SCENE 8 — THE ENDING**
So there you have it. Six simple habits that can help keep you stuck.
Of course, avoiding them won't magically make you rich. But changing even one might help you move forward.
Turn off the TV for one hour. Pick one hobby. Start the Monday project today. Do one scary thing a week. Make your own luck. And own what you can control.
Now, if you'll excuse me… I've got seventeen episodes of a show to finish. For research purposes, obviously.
