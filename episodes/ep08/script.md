# Ebo Teaches Money, Ep 08: Why Nice Guys Get Taken Advantage Of

Status: v3, approved 2026-11-09 — the Peanut-style story (house rules 20 and 21): a story about Ebo told deadpan in the
third person, concrete nouns that each become a picture on their word, escalating in threes, a twist, then the lesson in
two lines. Tracker: dinner (a receipt) · the move (a box) · the car (keys) · ? → the party, and the bill.
v1 (joke-led) is script_v1.md, v2 (bold and blunt) is script_v2_blunt.md.

EBO: Ebo was the nicest guy in his whole friend group.
EBO: He covered dinner when Christy forgot her wallet. Four times.
EBO: He helped Ama move. Twice. Same apartment.
EBO: He lent his car for "one hour." It came back three days later… with a parking ticket.
EBO: So for his birthday, they threw him a surprise party.
EBO: At his favorite restaurant.
EBO: And sent him the bill.
EBO: Ebo didn't get used because he was nice. He got used because his yes was free.
EBO: So now he says no. No speech.
EBO: And next birthday… he's ordering lobster.
