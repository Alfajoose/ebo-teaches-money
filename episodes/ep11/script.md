# Ep 11 — The Dangerous Side of Being Too Available

Draft 1, 2026-11-10. A bold life lesson (user's topic list). Rules 20–22: one story, explained step by step
(cause → effect → fix), Ebo in the FIRST PERSON (his own story, so he can be on camera and in the action),
characters speak in their own voices, pauses written in, one moral at the end. ~50 s at a brisk read.

**The question:** why do the people who are always there get taken for granted?
**The answer (one chain):** always available → your time looks free → free things get used, not valued →
people stop planning *with* you and plan *around* you → your own life becomes the gap between their calls.
**The fix:** be reachable, not on call. Give a time. Protect one thing that's yours.

Cast (CAST.md roles): Ebo; Ama (his wife, the sensible one); Mica (the friend who always asks);
Christy (the neighbour); Obuor (the boss, one line).
