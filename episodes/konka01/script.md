# Can't control your lust? (Konka, ~1:09)

**[0:00–0:08] THE HOOK**
So... you can't control your lust?
One attractive person walks past, and suddenly your brain forgets every important thing you were supposed to do today.
Congratulations. Your hormones have officially taken over management.

**[0:08–0:19] STEP 1 — FIND YOUR PURPOSE**
First, get yourself a purpose. Seriously. Find something you actually want to achieve.
Because when your entire day consists of doing absolutely nothing, your brain has plenty of time to entertain some very questionable ideas.

**[0:19–0:30] STEP 2 — WORK ON YOURSELF**
Second, work on yourself. Hit the gym. Learn a skill. Build something.
Give your brain something productive to obsess over.
And no, doing three push-ups and checking your abs in the mirror doesn't count as a transformation.

**[0:30–0:41] STEP 3 — CONTROL YOUR MIND**
Third, stop entertaining every thought that enters your head.
Just because your brain suggests something doesn't mean you have to follow through.
Your brain also tells you to eat an entire pizza at midnight. Doesn't mean it's a genius.

**[0:41–0:52] STEP 4 — AVOID YOUR TRIGGERS**
Fourth, stop feeding the temptation. You know that account you keep checking? Yeah. That one. Unfollow it.
You can't complain about being distracted while deliberately surrounding yourself with distractions.

**[0:52–1:05] STEP 5 — BUILD DISCIPLINE**
Finally, practice self-discipline. Start small. Set boundaries. Stick to a routine.
And remember, feeling attraction is completely normal. The goal isn't to become a robot.
It's to stop letting every passing impulse make your decisions.

**[1:05–1:09] ENDING**
Because if you can't control your attention... Something else will.
